Fair questions

Who are you, actually?
One person, US-based, building sites by hand. I answer the emails, I build the site, I take the calls. There's no team behind the word "we" — I use it out of habit, not deception.
Why so cheap compared to agencies?
No office, no account managers, no sales team. It's the same craft with less overhead — and CodeKitchen is new, so early pricing reflects that. It won't stay this low forever.
What if I hate the first draft?
Then I redo it, once, for free. If we're still not a fit after that, you pay nothing beyond a $100 deposit for the time spent. No hard feelings.
Can I update it myself?
Yes — everything's in your name with logins you hold. If you want me handling updates instead, that's what the care plan is for.
What do you need from me?
Your business name, what you do, any photos or logo you have, and a domain if you own one. That's genuinely it. I'll ask questions as I build.
