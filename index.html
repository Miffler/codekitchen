Questions

What does "Website Builder Agent" mean?
I run a system (Miffler/Hermes) that does the work: researches your market, generates custom images/video via Higgsfield AI, writes clean HTML/CSS/JS, runs headless QA, and deploys. I steer it. You get a site, not a template.
Why $0 today?
You don't pay until you've seen a working draft and approved it. If you don't like it, you walk away — no charge. The $200 deposit only happens after you say "yes, build it."
What if I don't have a domain?
We'll help you pick and register one (~$12/yr, in your name). You own it. If you already have one, we'll point it at the new site.
What happens after 30 days?
If you have the Care Plan, nothing changes — I keep hosting and maintaining. If not, you get a zip of your site + step-by-step instructions for Cloudflare Pages (free). Your domain stays in your name. Code is yours forever.
Can I see examples?
Every site is custom — no portfolio of "templates" to browse. The agent builds fresh each time. If you want to see the quality bar, get in touch and I'll share a recent build privately.
What stack do you use?
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No React, no WordPress, no builders. Fast, secure, zero dependencies. Deployed on Railway + Cloudflare. You can host anywhere.
